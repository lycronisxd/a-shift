<h1>instructions!</h1>
<h2>if you made it this far - congrats! it's time to build the shifter!</h2>
<hr>
<h3>what ya need!</h3>
<h4>3d printed parts</h4>
  <li>1 x <b>3d printed y-axis chamber</b></li>
  <li>2 x <b>3d printed x-axis bearing pegs</b></li>
  <li>1 x <b>3d printed y-axis bearing peg</b></li>
  <li>1 x <b>3d printed main chassis</b></li>
  <li>1 x <b>3d printed shift gate</b></li>
  <li>1 x <b>3d printed y-axis bearing holder</b></li>
  <li>1 x <b>3d printed shift knob</b></li>
  <li>1 x <b>3d printed clamp head</b></li>
  <li>1 x <b>3d printed clamp body</b></li>
<h4>bearings</h4>
  <li>4 x <b>608 series bearings</b></li>
  <li>2 x <b>625 series bearings</b></li>
<h4>mounting hardware</h4>
  <li>4 x <b>m3x5mm bolts</b></li>
  <li>8 x <b>m4 nuts</b></li>
  <li>8 x <b>m4 washers</b></li>
  <li>15 x <b>m4x10mm bolts</b></li>
  <li>2 x <b>m5 nuts</b></li>
  <li>2 x <b>m5x10mm bolts</b></li>
  <li>1 x <b>m10 washer</b></li>
  <li>1 x <b>m10 nut</b></li>
<h4>springs</h4>
  <li>2 x <b>25mm long, 5mm diameter springs</b></li>
  <li>2 x <b>45mm long, 5mm diameter springs</b></li>
  <li>1 x <b>20mm long, 11mm diameter spring</b></li>
<h4>misc.</h4>
  <li>1 x <b>200mm m10 threaded rod</b></li>
  <li>8 x <b>kw13-2 limit switches</b></li>
  <li>1 x <b>a-shift pcb</b></li>
</ul>

<p>(p.s - if you want to buy the bare pcb and assemble it yourself, <a href="https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1MjrU7IrbQ_ZQorYGkfbvgLpo6hidaAycoKFRKqDxL6c/edit?usp=sharing">here's a spreadsheet</a> with the components that i used and recommend for the pcb! :D)</p>
<hr>
<h3>how to build!</h3>
<ol>
  <li>assemble the <b><b>a-shift pcb</b></b> (if neccessary)</li>
  <li>flash the <b>a-shift pcb</b> firmware</li>
  <li>bolt the <b>a-shift pcb</b> to the <b>3d printed main chassis</b> with <b>m3x5mm bolts</b></li>
  <li>solder connections from the <b>kw13-2 limit switches</b> to the pcb pads</li>
  <li>test all of the switches on your computer</li>
  <li>superglue all switches to their respective slots</li>
  <li>drill an 8mm hole 50mm up on <b>200mm m10 threaded rod</b></li>
  <li>superglue <b>3d printed x-axis bearing pegs</b> to <b>3d printed y-axis chamber</b></li>
  <li>assemble <b>608 series bearings</b> in <b>3d printed y-axis chamber</b></li>
  <li>thread the <b>3d printed y-axis bearing peg</b> through both bearings and <b>200mm m10 threaded rod</b> hole</li>
  <li>put <b>m4 nuts</b> and <b>m4 washers</b> over each end of the <b>25mm long and 45mm long, 5mm diameter springs</b></li>
  <li>thread <b>m4x10mm bolts</b> in the back of the <b>3d printed y-axis chamber</b> holes and tighten <b>m4 nuts</b> until <b>all 4 springs</b> are secured to <b>3d printed y-axis chamber</b></li>
  <li>put the <b>3d printed y-axis chamber</b> pegs through the <b>3d printed main chassis</b> bearing holes without any bearings</li>
  <li>put <b>608 series bearings</b> on each side of the pegs and friction fit one by one into <b>3d printed main chassis</b> bearing holes</li>
  <li>thread <b>m4x10mm bolts</b> in the holes on the sides of the <b>3d printed main chassis</b> and tighten <b>m4 nuts</b> until <b>all 4 springs</b> are attached to the <b>3d printed main chassis</b></li>
  <li>superglue <b>m5 nuts</b> into both slots of the <b>3d printed y-axis bearing holder</b></li>
  <li>bolt <b>625 series bearings</b> through the <b>m5 nuts</b> on the <b>3d printed y-axis bearing holder</b> with <b>m5x10mm bolts</b></li>
  <li>slide <b>3d printed y-axis bearing holder</b> onto the <b>200mm m10 threaded rod</b> with bearings on top of the ramps of <b>3d printed y-axis chamber</b></li>
  <li>slide a <b>20mm long, 11mm diameter spring</b> over the <b>200mm m10 threaded rod</b> above the <b>3d printed y-axis bearing holder</b></li>
  <li>use a <b>m10 washer</b> and a <b>m10 nut</b> to secure the <b>20mm long, 11mm diameter spring</b> and <b>3d printed y-axis bearing holder</b> to the rod</li>
  <li>bolt the <b>3d printed shift gate</b> to the <b>3d printed main chassis</b> with <b>m4x10mm bolts</b></li>
  <li>test the shifter to see if it goes into each gear</li>
  <li>thread the <b>3d printed shift knob</b> over the <b>200mm m10 threaded rod</b></li>
  <li>thread the <b>3d printed clamp body</b> through the bottom a little bit</li>
  <li>superglue the <b>3d printed clamp head</b> to the <b>3d printed clamp body</b></li>
</ol>
